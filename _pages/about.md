Hi, I’m Mohadeseh Khoshnevisan, but I prefer to go by
**Mahi** (pronounced MAH-hee, just like the fish in mahi-mahi 🐟).

I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of
Colorado Boulder, advised by Prof. Jed Brown. I received my
BSc in Mathematics and Applications from Iran University of
Science and Technology.

My research interests include numerical methods for partial
differential equations, scientific computing, scientific machine
learning, and optimization.

My current work focuses on Riemann solvers for shallow-water
simulations in RDycore and numerical evaluation of quasiperiodic
Green’s functions for the Helmholtz equation.

[Read about my research](/research/)

## Teaching and mentoring

I’m a teaching assistant for CSCI 5229 – Computer Graphics
and a graduate mentor in CU Boulder’s Directed Reading Program.
